Capstone · Group 01 · screen flow v1

AlertAble — 21 screens

A disaster app built on one assumption: at the moment help is needed most, the network is down and the person cannot act. The phone broadcasts for them. Anyone nearby with the app can hear it.

1. Register once, calmly
2. Phone broadcasts quietly, always
3. Hazard hits
4. Companion is asked to confirm
5. No answer → barangay is alerted
6. Someone nearby follows the signal
A

Setup — done once, before anything happens

Registration happens at the barangay hall during enrollment, not during a disaster. This is where consent is taken, the assistance profile is set, and companions are named. Everything after this runs on its own.
